## RAISE A GLITCH
==================================


14/5/2026                4:04 PM

Hey, I’m finally done with raise a glitch, it was my first project and I made it with passion and love, the game may be buggy and glitchy but it still works hopefully, if not just revert the time in roblox studio. Below, you’ll find a brief overview of the game.


I started this project mid 6th grade when I was thinking about becoming a fullstack developer at roblox, I asked ai about some tips and it said making a roblox game so I decided to make a raise a floppa type of game (you can see the impressions of it in my game) and I decided to make a raise a gubby game, but I quickly scrapped it since someone already made a similar game and stuff (btw if you don’t know in raise a floppa type games work you get money from a thingy that oves around like a floppa or a dog and use it to buy upgrades and get more money and more upgrades etc.) I was inspired in the beginning by a game called raise a bob and again, you can see the impressions it left on my game. 

Now onto how I started, I learned luau from a course by brawldev (https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQ1Qd31Hmi3W_CGDzYOp7enyHlOuO3MtC)
(https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQ1Qd31Hmi3WKkVHnadvhOOjz04AuMYAf) I had a notebook to doodle and keep notes about learning luau which helped and lowk I struggled with tables a lot haha and in the end when I finished learning I decided to actually develop the game.

I started off by designing the house and the datastores and the actual glitch and the upgrades and by then I was done for the simple raise a floppa type game but I wanted more, I integrated upgrades into datastores (pain in the ass) and added npcs with custom dialogues. (If you go to starter gui and open the dialogue gui thing and then go into the dhandler thing it HAS ALL OF THE DIALOUGES) then I decided to make it into a psychological horror game, I tried to take inspiration from popular psychological horror games like let him go and happy world with happy people ^_^ but my main inspiration was duality. 

This was the downfall where I started to get burnt out and overwhelmed, after school I dreaded to work on it what if I didn’t get views or plays , what if this, what if that. I had a bunch of mental breakdowns when developing, (I hated adjusting music to days, fixing buggy and complicated scripts like the generator script music script day handler script, moon animator animations into scripts WHICH WAS THE WORST THERE WAS NO HELP ANYWHERE) and after a year almost I finished the game by drawing all of the thumbnails over and over again icons. And debugging for months. But in the end I told my friends and they kept pressuring me to release it and one day I snapped and just cried in my bed this was the time roblox began its shitty face introducing ai age verifications, age groups and more (I got put in <12 age group when I was 13 at the time which disabled a lot of features) and by then I was done. I removed the multiplayer feauture which caused so many bugs and released it. I was planning on buying 5000 robux to advertise it but thnakfully I didn’t (that cost almost 70 CAD).

I probaby missed a bajillion features in here but I really don’t care and after that I was done with roblox.
